Fuel Pressure Highs and Lows

2019 Fiesta 1.6 EcoBoost · FORScan logs, Sep 30 to Oct 3

Main takeaways

  • Too low: after a shift, the HP pump sometimes delivers nothing, about 1 in 4 times. The rail falls to tank pressure (~90 psi) at 100% pump command and the engine goes lean. It also happens parked in neutral. One of these set P0087.
  • Too high: never above the ~2,150 psi max desired. Staying high while coasting is normal. One odd jump with the pump commanded off.
  • Misfires: no link to the pressure events. Cyl 3 is weak mostly at idle, with the pressure on target.

Pressure running high

  • Max ~2,150 psi, the same as max desired. No over-pressure.
  • Above target for 1–3 s after shifts (~65 times). Normal: the rail only bleeds down through the injectors.
  • Stays high for 5–19 s when coasting with fuel off (8 times). Normal. Back on target within 1–2 s once fuel resumes.
  • 3 brief overshoots, 230–430 psi for ~1 s.
  • 1 odd jump with the pump commanded off (below).

A jump with the pump commanded off

Oct 3, 20 min drive · 12:30–12:58 · shift at 43 mph, then coasting
  1. Shift: actual follows target down to ~800 psi.
  2. Jumps to ~1,770 psi vs 940 target at 0–1% pump command. Only time in 4 logs.
  3. Coasting, fuel off: holds ~1,350 psi for 14 s. Normal.
  4. Fuel back on at idle: on target within ~1 s.

Point 2 is based on only 1–2 readings. It's either a bad reading or the pump delivering fuel it wasn't commanded to. A sticking control valve could cause both this and the drops, but one event isn't proof.

About the data

FORScan updates each value about every 1.2 s, and the lines between readings are filled in. Pressures are in psi, speeds in mph.

FRP_DSD / FRP
Target / actual rail pressure
FLPA
Low-pressure (tank pump) side
F_VCV
HP pump volume control valve command
CPP_PERCENT
Clutch pedal position
EQ_RAT11
Measured AFR (29.3 = fuel off)
FRPC_STAT
Rail pressure fault flag
CYL_n_ACCL
Misfire monitor, per cylinder (-1 or lower = weak)
